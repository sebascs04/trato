# 5. Refund part of a payment

After paying the store addendum, Café Aurora no longer needs two of its product pages and asks for USD 40 back.

| File | When to upload it |
| --- | --- |
| `client-refund-request-email.pdf` | As the freelancer, open **Refunds → Register refund request** and attach it as the client's proof |

The client can also ask from their own account with **Request a refund**, and then Trato saves that request as the proof. Step 7 of the [guide](../README.md#7-refund-part-of-a-payment) walks through both ways.
