# Confirmed initial agreement

Project: Café Aurora website
Client: Café Aurora, represented by Lucy Rivers
Freelancer: Alex Vega, web design and development

Date: November 5, 2026, 10:00 AM

## Agreed scope
Design and development of a responsive five-section website in English: Home, Menu, About us, Gallery and Contact. It includes an inquiry form, loading the content provided by Café Aurora, two rounds of revisions and launching the site.

Final delivery is agreed for November 20, 2026. Café Aurora will provide copy, photos and logo before development starts.

## Price and deliverables
The total project price is USD 600. There is no upfront payment. Each deliverable is paid after the client confirms it is complete:

- Deliverable 1, planning and initial UI design: USD 180 (30 %).
- Deliverable 2, website development: USD 300 (50 %).
- Deliverable 3, testing and launch: USD 120 (20 %), before the final version goes live.

## Out of scope
An online store, cart, payment integration, user accounts, inventory, coupons, translations and new sections are not included. Additional requests require a separate proposal stating scope, price and date, accepted by the client before that work begins.

## Early termination
If the project stops, both parties will review the work actually done and the payments received. Any payment for work not performed will be refunded. No fixed cancellation fee was agreed.
